# Bio and Novels by Iain Banks

### Bio

Iain Banks was a Scottish novelist known for writing both mainstream literary fiction (as Iain Banks) and science fiction (as Iain M. Banks). Born in Dunfermline in 1954, he studied at the University of Stirling and published his breakthrough novel, _++The Wasp Factory++_, in 1984. His science fiction is best known for the Culture series, set in a vast, post-scarcity interstellar civilization run largely by highly advanced artificial intelligences called Minds. Across his career, Banks earned critical acclaim for blending sharp political insight, dark humor, and large-scale space opera with intimate character work. He died in 2013, shortly after announcing he had terminal cancer.

### Non-Culture Novels

Beyond the Culture series, Iain Banks wrote a wide range of science fiction exploring darker, more self-contained futures. _++Against a Dark Background++_ follows Sharrow, a mercenary drawn back into a violent inheritance tied to a near-mythic weapon. _++Feersum Endjinn++_ is set in a far-future Earth ruled by a decaying aristocracy and features multiple viewpoints, including one written in phonetic dialect. _++The Algebraist++_ centers on a gas-giant civilization of ancient, inscrutable aliens whose secrets could shift galactic power. _++Transition++_ blends science fiction and thriller elements in a multiverse tale of agents who move between parallel Earths to manipulate political outcomes. Collectively, these novels retain Banks’s trademark mix of moral ambiguity, grand scale, and dark wit, but operate outside the Culture’s post-scarcity framework, often portraying harsher or more unstable civilizations.
